Privacy Policy

DarkSafari

Effective date: May 25, 2026

DarkSafari is a Safari extension for iPhone and iPad that blocks adult websites using on-device rules and page checks. This policy explains what data DarkSafari handles and how it is used.

Data collection

DarkSafari does not collect personal data. The app does not create accounts, does not use analytics, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share user data.

Browsing data

DarkSafari needs Safari extension access in order to compare website addresses and page signals against blocking rules. Website URLs, page titles, metadata, and visible page text may be processed locally on your device to decide whether a page should be blocked.

This browsing information is not sent to the developer, not uploaded to a server, and not used for advertising or tracking.

Local storage

DarkSafari may store extension settings locally on your device, such as whether protection is enabled and the built-in blocking configuration needed by Safari. This data stays on your device or in Apple's app container storage for the app and extension.

Tracking and advertising

DarkSafari does not track users across apps or websites. DarkSafari does not show ads and does not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.

Third-party services

DarkSafari does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

DarkSafari does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DarkSafari's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
