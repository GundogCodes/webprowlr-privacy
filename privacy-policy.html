Privacy Policy

WebProwlr

Effective date: November 20, 2026

WebProwlr is a browser extension for iPhone and iPad that blocks adult websites using on-device rules and page checks. This policy explains what data WebProwlr handles and how it is used.

Data collection

WebProwlr does not collect personal data. The app does not create accounts, does not use analytics, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share user data.

Browsing data

WebProwlr needs browser extension access in order to compare website addresses and page signals against blocking rules. Website URLs, page titles, metadata, and visible page text may be processed locally on your device to decide whether a page should be blocked.

WebProwlr does not send this browsing information to the developer or use it for advertising or tracking. On supported Google search pages, WebProwlr can add a SafeSearch preference to the Google URL. Google receives the search as it normally would when you use its website.

Local storage

WebProwlr stores your setup progress, search preference, personal block rules, exceptions, and latest Protection Check result per browsing mode on your device. Blocking preferences and test results are shared with the extensions. A check result records which tests passed, the time, and Safari's reported browsing mode. WebProwlr does not save a browsing history.

A new Protection Check replaces the previous result for that browsing mode. You can remove personal rules and exceptions in the app, revoke website access in Safari settings, or delete the app to remove its local data from the device. WebProwlr has no account or server-side browsing data to delete.

Protection Check

When you choose to run the check, Safari opens example.com and attempts two harmless requests to that same domain. These requests do not contain browsing history or personal rules. As with any website visit, the site can receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

Tracking and advertising

WebProwlr does not track users across apps or websites. WebProwlr does not show ads and does not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.

Third-party services

WebProwlr does not use third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

WebProwlr does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if WebProwlr's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
